Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

FitPreneur Agency ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use fitpreneuragency.com, how we use it, and the rights you have under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

FitPreneur Agency, 25 Cabot Square, Canary Wharf, London, E14 4QA. For any privacy question, email james@fitpreneuragency.com.

What we collect

  • Newsletter sign-ups: your name and email address.
  • Enquiries: anything you choose to send us by email or phone.
  • Technical data: basic, anonymised information such as browser type and pages visited, used to keep the site secure and working.

How we use it

  • To send our newsletter and free resources you asked for (based on your consent).
  • To reply to enquiries and provide our services (legitimate interests or contract).
  • To run and protect the website (legitimate interests).

Who we share it with

We use trusted providers to run our business, including MailerLite (email marketing) and Cloudflare (website hosting and security). They process data only on our instructions. We never sell your data.

How long we keep it

Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe. Enquiry data is kept for as long as needed to respond and for our legitimate business records.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your data, object to processing, or withdraw consent at any time. Every email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
