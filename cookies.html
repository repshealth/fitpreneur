Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. This page explains how fitpreneuragency.com uses them.

Cookies we use

  • Strictly necessary: our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may set cookies needed to keep the site secure and protect it from bots. These cannot be switched off.
  • Fonts: we load fonts from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address to deliver them.

We do not currently use advertising or tracking cookies. If we add analytics or advertising tools in future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect how the site works.
